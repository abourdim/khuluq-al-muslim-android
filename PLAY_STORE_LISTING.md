🌟 Khuluq al-Muslim — The Muslim's Character

Interactive trilingual app (Arabic / English / French) based on the book "The Muslim's Character" (Khuluq al-Muslim) by Sheikh Mohammed al-Ghazali (1917–1996).

📚 About the book

"The Muslim's Character" maps the core Islamic virtues across organized chapters: truthfulness, trustworthiness, modesty, mercy, justice, patience, forbearance, generosity of spirit, humility, and more. Each virtue gets its own chapter, with proofs from the Holy Quran and the authentic Sunnah.

✨ Features

• Each virtue presented as a structured card following the book's chapters
• Quranic verses and authentic hadith proofs for every virtue
• Three languages: Arabic, English, French
• Three color themes
• Reading mode, audio narrator, full RTL Arabic support
• Works offline — no internet required

🕌 Disclaimer

I am not a scholar. This is a humble effort by a Muslim parent to share Sheikh al-Ghazali's wisdom interactively. Content is derived from the book and trusted Islamic sources. This is not a fatwa.

📖 Sources

• "The Muslim's Character" (Khuluq al-Muslim) — Sheikh Mohammed al-Ghazali
• The Holy Quran and authentic Sunnah

May Allah have mercy on Sheikh al-Ghazali and reward him 🤲

« Le Caractère du Musulman » dresse la carte des grandes vertus islamiques au fil de chapitres organisés : véracité, probité, pudeur, miséricorde, justice, patience, indulgence, générosité d'âme, humilité, et bien d'autres. • Fonctionne hors ligne — aucune connexion requise
